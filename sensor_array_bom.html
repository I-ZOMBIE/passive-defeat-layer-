// FIELD FABRICATION GUIDE
POLE SENSOR ASSEMBLY (10 min each):
1. Cut coat hanger / rebar wire 350mm. Bend 20mm tab at 90° for base.
2. Epoxy or hot-glue PVDF film strip flat to base tab.
3. Solder 2 leads to film electrodes. Wrap in inner tube strip for weatherproofing.
4. Route leads along pole with zip ties. Cost: ~$0.80 film + free everything else.
FIBER TERMINATION (5 min each end):
1. Cut fiber to length. Hold end on 600-grit, rotate 30 sec. Repeat 2000-grit wet.
2. Press polished end face-to-face with BPW34 photodiode. Heat-shrink over both.
Side emission: run 400-grit lightly along length → light bleeds out sideways. Different LED color per zone = visual zone map. Free.
DIY BURST DISC (15 min):
1. Cut 20mm circle from 0.1mm brass shim OR wine bottle foil cap.
2. Score a cross with scalpel — light enough to hold, weak enough to pop on overpressure.
3. Clamp over polished fiber end with hose clamp or zip tie.
CONTROLLER BOX (5 min):
Any sealed food container. Drill cable entries, fill with hot glue or silicone. RPi inside.
Power: USB power bank / 4×AA + boost converter / dead drone LiPo + $1 BMS board.
